# Winner patterns (evidence thin: one documented podium project; see research-B)

1. **Operator tool with money at stake beats consumer chatbot.** TBO Assemble (3.0 1st runner-up, https://github.com/WarHawkADI/tbo-assemble) showed ₹ at risk from hotel contract penalties.
2. **Deployed, clickable demo with preloaded data.** TBO Assemble shipped five sample contracts for an instant demo.
3. **One quantified headline.** "3 days -> 30 minutes" (TBO Assemble).
4. **Model the industry's real rules** (penalty deadlines, GST, contract terms). Amadeus Trav-Tech 2025 judge: "intelligence without understanding the rules simply won't work" (research-B).
5. **GenAI turns messy documents into structured data that drives a workflow**, rather than chat for its own sake (TBO Assemble).
6. **Near-market readiness** (Amadeus Trav-Tech 2025 winner Trip.com "Click & Go").
7. **Travel-agent copilots appear every edition**, so the space is crowded (research-B repo survey).
8. India realism (₹, WhatsApp, GST): assumption, supported by the single podium project.
